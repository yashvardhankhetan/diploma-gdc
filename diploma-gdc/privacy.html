Privacy Policy

Diploma GDC · Last updated 27 August 2026

Diploma GDC collects no data of any kind. It has no account system, makes no network requests, contains no analytics or advertising, and includes no third-party code. Everything you type stays on your device.

That is the whole policy. The sections below simply say the same thing precisely enough to be relied on.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, receive, generate or transmit any personal information. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • Account credentials — there are no accounts
  • Location, contacts, photos, calendars, microphone or camera data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or IP addresses
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, diagnostics or telemetry
  • The expressions, notes, graphs or data you enter

Information stored on your device

The app saves your work — notebooks, stored variables, lists, saved graphs and your settings — so that it is still there when you come back. This is written to the app's own private storage on your device using the operating system's standard mechanisms.

We cannot see it. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. It is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup only if you have turned that on for the app, in which case it is governed by Apple's terms and remains inaccessible to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests. It contains no code that opens a network connection, no embedded web content, and no outbound links. There is no account system of any kind. It works with the device in Airplane Mode, and works identically on a device that has never been connected to a network.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no third-party libraries, software development kits, analytics services, advertising networks, or trackers. No data is shared or sold, because no data exists to share or sell.

Children

The app is intended for students studying the IB Diploma Programme, who are typically 16 to 18 years old, but it is safe for any age. Because it collects no information whatsoever from any user, it collects none from children. It contains no advertising, no user-generated content, no messaging, and no links out of the app.

Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or erase. The work saved on your device is under your control and is removed by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app were ever to collect anything, this policy would be updated before that version was released, and the change would be described in the app's release notes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to diplomagdc@gmail.com.
